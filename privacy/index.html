Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Borb is a social birding app that lets you build a personal bird collection, upload photos, record sightings, and share discoveries with other birders.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Borb collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Borb, we may collect:

  • Account information: information used to create and maintain your account, such as your email address, username, and profile information.
  • Bird sightings and collection data: species you add to your collection, sighting dates, locations, and other information you choose to record.
  • Photos: bird photos that you choose to upload to Borb.
  • Location information: locations that you choose to associate with your bird sightings.
  • Social content: profile information, sightings, photos, and other content that you choose to share with other Borb users.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain Borb.
  • Create and manage your account.
  • Save and display your bird collection and sightings.
  • Allow you to upload and manage bird photos.
  • Provide social and community features.
  • Allow other users to view content that you choose to share.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability and functionality of Borb.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback.

Public and Shared Content

Borb includes social features. Information that you choose to make public or share with other users may be visible to other Borb users.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in content that you choose to share publicly.

Service Providers

Borb uses third-party service providers to operate the app and securely process or store information.

  • Supabase: Borb uses Supabase for backend infrastructure and data storage. Information associated with your account and use of Borb may be processed or stored through Supabase.
  • Cloudflare: Borb uses Cloudflare infrastructure to store and deliver user-uploaded photos. Photos you upload may therefore be processed or stored using Cloudflare services.

These providers process information as necessary to provide their services to Borb and are subject to their own privacy and security practices.

Sharing of Information

Borb does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers such as Supabase and Cloudflare when necessary to operate the app.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Borb, its users, or others.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Borb and operate its features.

You may delete your Borb account using the account deletion feature within the app. When an account is deleted, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate, except where retention is required for legal, security, or operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

Borb is not intended for children under the minimum age required to consent to the processing of personal information in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent would be legally required.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored and processed through Borb. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Borb evolves. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please contact us at:

borbsupport@gmail.com
