Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Borb is a social birding app that lets you build a personal bird collection, upload photos, record sightings, and share discoveries with other birders.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Borb may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Borb, we may collect information such as:

  • Account information: information used to create and maintain your Borb account, such as your email address, username, or profile information.
  • Bird sightings and collection data: species you add to your collection, sighting information, dates, and other details you choose to record.
  • Photos: bird photos that you choose to upload to Borb.
  • Location information: locations that you choose to associate with your bird sightings.
  • Social content: posts, profile information, sightings, photos, and other content that you choose to share with other Borb users.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Borb app.
  • Save and display your bird collection and sightings.
  • Allow you to upload and manage your bird photos.
  • Provide Borb's social and community features.
  • Allow other users to view content that you choose to share.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of Borb.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback.

Public and Shared Content

Borb includes social features. Information that you choose to make public or share with other users may be visible to others through the app.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in content that you choose to share publicly.

Sharing of Information

Borb does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate Borb, such as hosting, storage, authentication, or other technical infrastructure. These providers may only process information as necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Borb, its users, or others.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide Borb and operate its features.

You may delete your Borb account using the account deletion feature within the app. When an account is deleted, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate, except where retention is required for legal, security, or operational purposes.

Children's Privacy

Borb is not intended for children under the minimum age required to consent to the processing of personal information in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent would be legally required.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored and processed through Borb. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Borb evolves. When changes are made, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.
